title: Granola
---

## Ingredients

- `660g` rolled oats
- `75g` wheat germ
- `7g` salt
- `5g` cinnamon or cardamom
- `140g` olive oil
- `160g` maple syrup
- vanilla
- ~`150g` nuts (pecans and/or walnuts)

## Notes
Mix oats, wheat germ, salt, spice(s) in a big bowl. Whisk wet ingredients together and then stir well into grains. Chop nuts and add them last.

Spread over 2 baking trays. Bake at 350 for 40 minutes. Watch for burning; rotate if needed. **Don't stir.** Allow to cool before breaking up.

## Source
This is heavily adapted from Deborah Madison's *Vegetarian Cooking for Everyone*.
