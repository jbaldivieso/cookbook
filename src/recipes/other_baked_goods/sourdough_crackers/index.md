---
title: Sourdough Crackers
---

## Ingredients

- `170g` sourdough starter, hot
- `50g` all-purpose flour
- `50g` whole wheat flour
- `25g` olive oil
- `2g` salt
- salt, herbs, etc for sprinkling

## Timing
- Mix together with starter that's been fed overnight.
- Let sit as a ball for up to 3 hours.
- Roll out, dress, cut, and then bake for 20 minutes at 325.

## Notes
I think it's helpful to mix the oil in with the starter and mix the dry ingredients together separately first. Roll out on parchment paper, real thin. Paint a thin layer of water on so the sprinkles stick. We've used 40g pecorino and black pepper, too. Surely herbs would be good, seeds, etc.

## Source
This is Maurizo Leo's "Sourdough Starter Crackers" from *The Perfect Loaf* (the book, not the website).
