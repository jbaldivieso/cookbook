---
title: Breadsticks (Grissini)
---

## Ingredients

### Levain
- `4g` sourdough starter, hot
- `36g` all-purpose flour
- `36g` water

### Dough
- Levain
- `250g` all-purpose flour
- `35g` olive oil
- `6g` superfine sugar
- `6g` salt

## Timing
- Mix levain together.
- 12 hours: overnight on counter
- Mix everything else in mixer with paddle. Low for 1 min, then medium for 3 min until clings to paddle. Rest for 5 minutes, then 2 min on medium until it clings again. **Shape** (see notes). [30 minutes]
- 3 hours: proof
- **Cut** (see notes) and bake for about 25 minutes at 350. Shooting for "golden and crunchy". [35 minutes]

## Notes
**Shape**:  he says to paint a strip of parchment paper with olive oil and then roll it out on that to 3"x14". Paint the top with olive oil, then bag the whole tray for proofing.

**Cut**: using bench scraper, cut strips 1/2" x 3". Stretch to tray's width (short dim) and place on a new strip of parchment paper. Space 1/2" apart.

## Source
This is Maurizo Leo's "Crunchy Breadsticks (Grissini)" from *The Perfect Loaf* (the book, not the website).
