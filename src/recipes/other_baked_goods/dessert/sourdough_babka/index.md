---
title: Sourdough Babka
---

## Ingredients
### Levain
- `45g` ripe starter
- `45g` water
- `45g` all-purpose white flour

### Dough
- the levain
- `2` medium eggs
- `105g` milk
- `310g` all-purpose white flour
- `30g` superfine sugar
- `8g` salt
- `100g` butter, cut into pats; **at room temp**

### Filling
- `40g` melted butter
- `175g` dark brown sugar
- `12g` flour
- `6g` cinnamon

## Timing
- Levain mix.
- 3 hours: rest
- Mix dough. See notes. [7 minutes]
- 10 minutes: rest
- Mix butter. See notes. [10 minutes]
- 1 hour: bulk fermentation.
- Fold
- 3 hours: bulk fermentation at warm temp. Maybe longer if it's cool.
- Wrap for fridge
- 12 hours ± 3: overnight rest in fridge
- Mix up filling. Cut out a parchment paper hammock. Roll out the dough on floured surface to 10"x24". Spread filling, leaving an inch on one long end. Roll tightly and seal at the end. [10 minutes]
- 10 minutes: freeze
- On parchment paper, slice down the length. Carefully braid, and pinch both ends together. Scrunch it down so it fits into pan (and put it in there). [10 minutes]
- 5 hours: proof. Maybe longer if not warm.
- Bake for an 40-45 minutes at 350. Internal temp should be 205. [45 minutes]


## Notes
This skips his proposed simple syrup glaze and egg wash. Def plenty sweet as is.

**Initial mix**: dough hook and mixer. Mix starter, milk, egg, flour, salt, sugar on low for 1-2 mins until combined, then medium for 3-4 mins until "dough clinks to dough hook".

**Butter mix**: low speed, add one pat at a time until absorbed into dough. 5-8 minutes to get all butter incorporated. Medium for another 1-2 minutes until "dough smooths out abd clings to the dough hook once again. The dough will be silky smooth, elastic, and a little shiny."


## Source
This is Maurizo Leo's "Cinnamon Babka" from *The Perfect Loaf* (the book, not the website).
