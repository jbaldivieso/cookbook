---
title: Raisin-Pecan Bread
---

## Ingredients

- `100g` starter
- `380g` raisin-soaking tea/water
- `75g` rye flour
- `50g` whole-wheat flour
- `375g` white flour
- `100g` raisins (pre-soaked in Earl Grey)
- `11g` salt
- `3g` yeast
- `80g` pecans

## Timing
- Mix all but the pecans. [5 minutes]
- 15-20 minutes: autolyse
- Proper mix. Pecans go in halfway through. [5 minutes]
- 10 minutes: rest
- First fold
- ~30 minutes: rest, until "you see the dough spread out in the tub"
- Fold again
- ~2 hours 30 minutes: rest, until the dough is 2.5-3x original size
- Shape and move to pan. [10 minutes]
- 12 hours ± 3: overnight rest in fridge
- Bake for 50 minutes at 425 (or 400?). Cover with foil after 15-20 minutes. [50 minutes]

## Notes

Recipe calls for 50/50 water/tea; I just use all the tea I have. I often add a little more pecans. I sometimes replace 25g white flour with buckwheat.

## Source
This is Ken Forkish's "Raisin-Pecan Bread" from *Evolutions in Bread*.
