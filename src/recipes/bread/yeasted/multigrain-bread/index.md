title: Multigrain Bread
---

## Ingredients

- `100g` starter
- `295g` water (soaking liquid + hot water)
- `15g` maple syrup (or honey)
- `120g` whole-wheat flour
- `40g` rolled oats (soak first)
- `40g` rolled barley (soak first)
- `40g` buckwheat groats (soak first)
- `280g` white flour
- `14g` salt
- `3g` yeast
- `15g` oats (for sprinkling on top before baking)

## Timing
- Initial mix. [5 minutes]
- 15-20 minutes: autolyse
- Proper mix. [5 minutes]
- 10 minutes: rest
- First fold
- ~30 minutes: rest, until "you see the dough spread out in the tub"
- Fold again
- ~2 hours 30 minutes: rest, until the dough is 2.5-3x original size
- Shape and move to pan. [10 minutes]
- Bake for an hour (takes 10 minutes longer than other yeasted ones in Ken's book) at 425. Cover with foil after 15-20 minutes. [1 hour]

## Notes
Can mix and match with the grains. Quinoa works, too. Add them up to 120g. Soaking them in about 250g of water yields about the right amount of leftover liquid.

## Source
This is Ken Forkish's "Multigrain Bread" from *Evolutions in Bread*.
