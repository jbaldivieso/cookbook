---
title: Pain de campagne
---

## Ingredients

- `200g` starter (hot)
- `300g` water (90-95˚)
- `25g` whole or dark rye flour
- `80g` whole-wheat flour
- `295g` white flour
- `11g` salt

## Timing
- Initial mix. [5 minutes]
- 20 minutes: autolyse
- Proper mix. [5 minutes]
- 10 minutes: rest
- First fold
- ~30 minutes: rest, until "you see the dough spread out in the tub"
- Fold again
- ~30 minutes: rest, until "you see the dough spread out in the tub"
- Fold again
- ~2 hours: rest, until the dough is 2.5 original size
- Shape and move to brotform. [10 minutes]
- 12 hours ± 3: overnight rest in fridge
- Bake at 450 in closed dutch oven for 40 minutes, then 15 minutes with lid off at 400-425. [55 minutes]


## Source
This is Ken Forkish's "Country Bread, EIB-style" from *Evolutions in Bread*. He includes a walnut variation.
